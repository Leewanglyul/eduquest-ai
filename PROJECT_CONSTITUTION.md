# EduQuest Project Constitution

Status: approved by the PM (2026-11-02). Level 1 of the governance hierarchy ([docs/governance](docs/governance/README.md)).
Every feature, design and analytics decision follows it.

## Product context (PM clarifications, 2026-11-02)

- **Users:** middle-school students first, high-school students second; teachers are first-class users. Elementary-school
  assumptions do not drive governance decisions.
- **Environment:** a teacher-operated classroom, not an open online social platform. Flow: teacher → Course Room →
  teams → students. The teacher is present and may intervene during play.
- **Unit:** the **Course Room** (a classroom, an elective course, a high-school credit course, a club, a workshop).
- **Competition:** individual competition is discouraged; teacher-controlled team competition is allowed and
  configurable.
- **Growth:** improvement compared to one's previous self, measured through recall, review, spaced repetition and
  personal mastery, not collaborative completion alone.

## Core value order

**Growth > Achievement > Collaboration > Competition**

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Students are protected.
2. Teachers diagnose.
3. Growth is measured through personal recall.
4. Rankings are prohibited.
5. Direct student comparison is prohibited.
6. Collaboration should be encouraged but not forced.

**Interpretation of rules 4 and 5 (PM, 2026-11-02).** Individual student rankings and direct individual comparison are
prohibited. Teacher-configured team comparison may be enabled for instructional purposes under the projection-safety
rules ([Operational UX Principles](docs/governance/operational-ux-principles-v1.md) OX-5, OX-12).

## Student identity (PM, 2026-11-03)

Student identity is persistent and personal. Teachers may manage student access within Course Rooms, but personal
growth records are associated with the student's persistent internal identity. QR codes and join links are access
conveniences, not the identity of the student. Personal growth records are not associated primarily with a nickname,
a device, a QR code, a temporary join code, a team, a session, an attempt, an assignment or a Course Room; Course Room
membership may change, but the personal association of the growth record remains.

This principle does not by itself approve cross-Course-Room access, successor-teacher access, school-year transfer,
transfer between schools, long-term retention, student or guardian access, or erasure and account-merging behaviour;
each needs its own data-governance decision.

## Governance rule

Any future collaboration proposal must:

1. reference the [Collaboration Governance Package](docs/governance/eduquest-collaboration-governance-package-v1.md);
2. identify conflicts before proceeding;
3. explain its alignment with the approved principles.
